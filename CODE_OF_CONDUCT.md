# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## What is expected

- Treat people with respect, including when you think they are wrong.
- Give criticism about the work, with reasons. "This has a nonce-reuse bug on line 40" is
  useful; "this is garbage" is not.
- Accept criticism about your own work the same way.
- Respect that contributors have different levels of experience, different first languages,
  and different amounts of free time.
- Respect privacy. Many people working on tools like this have good reasons not to use their
  legal name. Do not ask, do not speculate, do not out anyone.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments
- Discrimination on any basis
- Sexual language or imagery
- Publishing anyone's private information
- Deliberately disruptive behaviour or sustained bad faith
- Pressuring anyone to reveal their identity, location or employer

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any other project space,
plus any situation where someone is representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers at *add your contact address*. Reports are handled
privately. Responses range from a warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity and
pattern. Maintainers are held to the same standard, more strictly rather than less.
